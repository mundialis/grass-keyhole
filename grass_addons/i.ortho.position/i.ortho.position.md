## DESCRIPTION

*i.ortho.position* estimates the camera postion from the corner
coordinates of the scene in longitudes and latitudes, omega and flight
height.

The estimated parameters are kappa and the coordinates of the camera at
the time of exposure. These values can be used for *i.ortho.init*
